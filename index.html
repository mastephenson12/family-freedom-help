01 / LOGIN & ACCESS

I purchased a product. Where do I log in?

Start with your purchase confirmation or welcome email. Use the access link provided for that product and the email address you used at checkout. Product access can vary by purchase.

If your purchase includes Digital Growth Community access, follow your invitation to join. Already a member? Open Digital Growth Community on Skool.

I can’t find my welcome or access email.

Check your inbox, spam, junk, and promotions folders. Search for your product name, purchase receipt, “Digital Growth Community,” or “Skool.” Confirm you are checking the email address used at checkout.

If you still cannot find it, contact Mark with your purchase email, product name, and order reference if available.

I forgot my password or used a different email.

Use the password-reset option on the login page for the platform where your product is hosted. Check the inbox associated with that account. If your checkout email and login email differ, include both when requesting help so your access can be investigated.

Never send your password or one-time login codes to support.

02 / PRODUCTS & GETTING STARTED

How do I know which products I have access to?

Your order confirmation identifies what you purchased. Compare it with the products or courses visible in your account. A community membership does not necessarily include every separately sold product.

If a purchased item is missing, send Mark the product name and order reference. You do not need to buy it again to ask for help.

Where should I begin inside the community?

Look for the current welcome, onboarding, or “start here” material in your account. Read the community guidelines and follow the learning path for your purchase. Use the community’s current announcements and calendar for session details.

For questions about your specific access or purchase, contact Mark below.

Does purchasing a product guarantee income?

No. Educational products and community participation do not guarantee earnings or a particular outcome. Your results depend on factors including your experience, effort, decisions, and market conditions.

03 / TECHNICAL HELP

A lesson, video, or download won’t open.

Refresh the page and confirm that you are signed in to the correct account. Try an updated browser or a private browsing window, and check your internet connection. If a download is involved, check your browser’s downloads folder.

If the problem continues, send the page or lesson name, the exact error, your browser and device, and what you have already tried. A screenshot can help; remove any private information first.

A link is broken or I see an access error.

Use the original access link from your welcome email and make sure it belongs to the product you purchased. If it still fails, copy the page address and error message into your support request. Do not include private sign-in links, passwords, or verification codes.

PLEASE REVIEW BEFORE PURCHASING

All sales are final.
No refunds.

The Family Freedom Plan does not offer refunds on Digital Growth Community products purchased through this website. Review the product description, inclusions, and checkout terms before completing your purchase.

Questions before purchasing? Ask Mark first. If you have an access or technical issue after purchasing, we can help you troubleshoot it.

I have an access problem. Can I still get help?

Yes. The no-refunds policy does not prevent you from requesting access or technical support. Contact Mark with your purchase details and a description of the issue.

My receipt or billing details don’t look right.

Contact Mark with the product name, purchase date, order reference, and a brief description of the discrepancy so it can be reviewed. Do not email a full payment-card number, security code, or bank credentials.
